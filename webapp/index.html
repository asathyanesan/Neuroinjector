UD Neuroinjector — Syringe Configurator

Pick the Hamilton syringe model(s) you use, then download a ready-to-flash injector_control.ino. Everything runs locally in your browser — no data is sent anywhere.

1. Choose your syringe(s)

These share the 7.8 mm barrel OD used by the current 3D-printed housing and drop straight in. Check every syringe you want available from the device's Select Syringe menu (up to 7).

2. (Optional) Add a syringe not listed

Have a Hamilton syringe that isn't in the list, or a non-Hamilton syringe? Enter its rated volume and plunger stroke length (see its datasheet), or directly type its nL/mm dispense rate if you already know it.
